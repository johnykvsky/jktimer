JK Timer is a fast, clean, and customizable interval timer for Android — designed for HIIT, Tabata, circuit training, calisthenics, boxing, and structured workout routines.

Features:
• Simple & Advanced Modes: Set quick work/rest intervals or create custom multi-step routines with up to 99 exercises and custom labels (e.g. Pushups, Plank).
• Glanceable Visuals: Large animated countdown ring, alternating colors for consecutive workouts, and "Next Up" exercise previews.
• Sound & Haptics: Distinct audio cues for interval transitions, 10-second warnings, and 3-2-1 countdown beeps with adjustable volume and vibration.
• Background & Lock Screen Support: Runs reliably via foreground notifications when the screen is locked or while using other apps.
• Bilingual UI: Native support for English and Polish with automatic system detection and in-app language switching.
• Landscape Mode: Adaptive 2-column layout for tablets and widescreen phones.
• 100% Offline & Private: Zero internet permissions, no telemetry, no tracking, and no ads.
